Competition: news-image-20261122, "Sum up 22 November's world
news in one image" ($100 pool). Deliverable: one whole PNG or

Why this build exists: the 22-story dense page built earlier
the same morning (entries/news-image-20261122-v2) carried the
full day's coverage but read cramped and uneven. Jaka's
direction for this revision: "lebih di rapihkan lagi dan di

"jangan acak acakan gi" (don't make it messy again) and "kasih
arahan setiap berita biar pembaca tidak bingung" (give every
story a guiding cue so readers are never confused). So this
page keeps the coverage and rebuilds the presentation:
strict grid first, story count second.

## Design and reader guides

One square page, one reading path, five bands separated by
thin rules: masthead; lead; two equal secondary boxes; four
equal panels; a split band (IN BRIEF list on the left, a
uniform 2x2 mini-panel grid and the SPORT strip on the right);
black footer bar. Every panel follows the identical internal
order — engraving, kicker, headline, detail line — and the
lead and secondary boxes put the kicker directly above the
headline, so the guide system never changes its shape.

The kickers are the page's orientation system: SOUTH AMERICA
on the lead; MIDDLE EAST and EUROPE on the secondary boxes;
EUROPE, LATIN AMERICA, CLIMATE, EUROPE on the panels; SCIENCE,
ASIA, MIDDLE EAST, LATIN AMERICA on the mini-panels; EUROPE
inside the footer bar; SPORT labelling the sport strip. Each
brief additionally opens with its own place lead-in. Type is
kept to four sizes (masthead, headlines, detail lines,
kickers/list), all bold condensed capitals, with white space
inside every box. Nothing on the page is slanted, overlapped
or decorated beyond the engravings themselves, and no
lettering appears inside any illustration.
